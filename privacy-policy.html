Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, we may process:

We use the information described above only to provide and operate the automation features you request, including:

  • Reading and responding to social-media interactions necessary for the configured automation.
  • Sending or publishing authorized content when the account owner enables such functionality.
  • Monitoring errors, preventing abuse, and maintaining the security and reliability of the service.
